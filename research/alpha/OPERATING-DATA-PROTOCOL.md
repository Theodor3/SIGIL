# Small alternative-data experiments

Main track: unusual public operating data for sustained business improvement and
longer-term investing. Faster event-driven trades are a separate research track.
These are proposed tests; no signal or investment ranking is activated.

## First question: freight recovery quality

Does an eight-week improvement in service, alongside resilient shipment volume,
precede improved company operating performance after controlling for seasonality,
peer conditions, traffic mix, fuel and labor costs? Only then ask whether the
information adds anything to a valuation-aware 6/12-month investment screen.

The prototype binds one actual CSX workbook to its source cells: October10–16
observation period, retrieved October27 at07:32:58UTC. Original public release
time is unknown. Its October21 filename is neither the observation period nor
verified information availability. No rolling trend can be estimated from this
single bound packet, and the older report-only audits are not a substituted panel.

Before collecting/looking at test outcomes, specify and review: operator universe
and stable terminal identities; exact speed/dwell/commodity units and signs;
the eight-week rule and missing-week policy; own-seasonal and peer baselines;
revisions and raw-vintage retention; price/valuation measures known at each date;
forecast timestamp and entry convention; 6/12-month horizon; 10/25/50bps assumed
round-trip costs; capex, fuel, labor and mix controls; all attempted variants.
No individual shipper/customer/supplier mapping is supported by this packet.

Preserve each raw response locally with URL, original bytes/hash, retrieved UTC,
observation period, verified publication time if available, and verification
evidence. When publication is unknown, use first receipt prospectively rather
than backdating availability. The STB describes weekly carrier data and a
harmonized service compilation in its official announcement:
https://www.stb.gov/news-communications/latest-news/pr-22-54/
This establishes the source program, not timestamps for each workbook.

Falsifiers: lower dwell driven by falling volume; no incremental business KPI
improvement versus controls; apparent effect vanishes after seasonal adjustment,
valuation or cost assumptions; concentrated results from one event/operator;
unavailable source or outcome coverage. Preserve each negative conclusion.
Eight weekly observations are a collection gate, not eight independent trials.

## Cheap experiment funnel

Flights, parking-lot counts, e-commerce activity and tanker port/draft data are
unprioritized ideas, not discovered alpha. Each first needs a lawful supported
source, actual availability/version evidence, defensible company exposure and a
small unit/coverage/timing audit. Avoid subscriptions, credentials changes,
large downloads or model/API calls under the expired authorization.

Freeze one candidate/rule per test, separate discovery from untouched prospective
evaluation, record every attempted variant, compare against simple baselines,
and deepen only after evidence survives costs and independent review. Positive
backtests alone do not establish future profitability. Keep bulky raw datasets
and experiment outputs outside Git and Railway; commit only reviewed code,
small sanitized source packets and concise manifests.
