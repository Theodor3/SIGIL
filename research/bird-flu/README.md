# Bird-flu and egg-supply research: exploratory, not a production signal

This local research tested the proposed secondary effect: outbreaks that reduce competing egg supply may help unaffected producers, while an issuer's own losses may hurt it. Own losses and industry scarcity are different exposures. No company was assumed unaffected without contemporaneous evidence. No live trading input or strategy was changed.

## Retained small disclosure cohort
Five predefined Cal-Maine disclosures, including negative/overlapping cases, were retained. It is a retrospective convenience sample, not an exhaustive outbreak registry or unseen holdout. The December2023 result was already known before this panel was assembled. Entries use the next common trading-session close strictly after disclosure date; exits are five common-session intervals later. Initial reaction is separate.

These are adjusted component-return differences in percentage points BEFORE costs, not executable self-financing spread returns. Current Yahoo-adjusted prices were not independently certified against a second price source: one attempted public Stooq check returned403 and was not bypassed. Price vintages are not original point-in-time snapshots. No pooled strategy performance, significance or validated alpha follows.

Five disclosures are not five independent outbreak shocks: four overlap earnings and the loss updates involve two geographic episode groups (Kansas/Texas), with repeated cumulative figures. A geographic group is not a biological outbreak census. FOMC/macro events also confound the December2023 window. There is no verified unaffected listed peer. A currently available annual filing about another producer cannot retroactively establish its contemporaneous exposure.

The initial own-loss-negative idea failed in the December2023 case. Retaining that counterexample and January2024 negative result is important; the panel does not justify reversing the rule after seeing prices.

## Supply-chain pilot: negative result retained
A separate frozen simple all-layer YoY contraction proxy, using small NASS report coverage and retail egg prices, achieved2/8directional matches in evaluation versus6/8for the predeclared price-momentum baseline. The feature had no variation in evaluation; the minimum10-observation gate also failed. All-layer counts are not a clean table-egg disease exposure, public retail prices are not wholesale producer realizations, and publication/vintage gaps prevent an investable historical test. The weak result is not a reason to relabel a retuned strategy as out-of-sample.

Public APHIS/AMS data could improve the hypothesis, but a dated original-vintage outbreak/replenishment/wholesale-inventory panel was not established. Current dashboards and later-downloaded files are not proof of historical release timing. No credentials or paid dataset were configured.

## Evidence and next falsifier
`disclosure-registry.json` preserves the fixed five issuer cases, definitions and primary-source links. `source-manifest.json` preserves URLs, SHA256/length and retrieval metadata only. Original issuer/USDA PDFs and third-party price arrays remain private/local and are not in this repository. Source references allow an authorized researcher to retrieve the evidence; this document is not a fully portable return backtest. No invented source history or license grant.

Primary cases include https://calmainefoods.gcs-web.com/node/13091/pdf and https://calmainefoods.gcs-web.com/node/13456/pdf; the full registry identifies every case, including repeat/earnings flags. Six synthetic panel tests and six supply-pilot tests passed in restricted Docker during local research, and independent arithmetic/source reviews were recorded. These checks verify calculations and provenance handling, not profitability.

Next useful experiment: predeclare an exhaustive dated issuer-disclosure set, distinguish first occurrence from cumulative repeats, bind each event to contemporaneous company exposure and egg-price release vintages, flag earnings/macro overlap, and compare against sector/market benchmarks with trading costs only after timestamp and sample gates pass. Preserve null/negative cases. No automated signal or trade is authorized by this note.
