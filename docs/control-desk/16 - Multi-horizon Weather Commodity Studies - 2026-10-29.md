# Multi-horizon weather and commodity studies

A neural network is a candidate estimator, not an investment strategy. First ask a narrow falsifiable question: do genuinely issued weather forecast revisions improve 1-, 5- and 10-trading-day Henry Hub price-change predictions beyond price-only baselines? Gas demand provides a direct weather mechanism; crop weather remains a parallel research track with separate outcomes and geography. Theo prefers crops for weather research, so the gas study is a candidate benchmark, not an automatic replacement of the ongoing crop work. Neither is data-ready for tradable-return testing.

## Freeze one target and price source before fitting
Primary proposed family: named Henry Hub delivery-contract future price changes at h=1,5,10 exchange trading sessions. Define reference cutoff, earliest executable entry after data receipt, units USD/MMBtu, exact venue/contract/year/delivery, session calendar, bid/ask or settlement semantics and roll exclusion before acquiring outcomes. Choose only contracts with sufficient maturity through the full label horizon. Do not splice a back-adjusted rolling continuous future or quietly change contract as an existing label matures. One-day horizon is the primary comparison; 5/10-day are prespecified secondary results with multiplicity disclosed.

Existing audit found no verified licensed named-contract history in inspected local locations. Existing keys, FMP paid access or HTTP200 instrument-directory metadata do not prove entitlement to contract-level history; the corn history probe returned402. Do not purchase or expand credential access. If rights-cleared contract prices remain absent, choose a separately registered non-investable study using properly dated daily Henry Hub spot observations, or prospective collection after authorization. Monthly EIA/BLS data cannot answer 1/5/10-session futures returns. Spot prices are not executable futures fills. Fallback must be chosen before outcomes, never after seeing which target performs better.

## Feature and availability contract
For each raw item retain source/rights, source cycle, forecast initialization, valid interval, member, grid/version, retrieval start/end UTC, SHA256, issue/publication evidence and available_at. Record revisions separately; never overwrite originals. Earliest availability is not inferred from observation date or today's download. A conservative archive-modification lag may support exploratory reconstruction only, with sensitivity tests and explicit uncertainty; strongest test uses prospective first-seen receipts.

Two consecutive actually issued cycles must forecast the SAME future valid windows. Compute revisions to demand-weighted HDD/CDD (declare base temperature/weights and weights' own availability), ensemble dispersion and fixed regional temperature summaries. Use only members/fields fully available at cutoff; record no-forecast cases rather than substituting later complete cycles. Historical weather model changes require version strata. Daily high/low or accumulated precipitation must use proper step intervals, not instantaneous fields or overlapping sums. Existing GFS local parser/receipt validators can be reused; numerical crop pair capture does not by itself create a gas-demand-weighted ensemble dataset.

NOAA explicitly distinguishes retrospective GEFSv12 reforecasts from archived operational forecasts: https://noaa-gefs-retrospective.s3.amazonaws.com/Description_of_reforecast_data.pdf . The reforecast archive is unsuitable as evidence that a forecast was available to investors at its historical initialization date. It can be used for declared weather-model development, never silently substituted for real-time vintages. Operational AWS archive is a candidate requiring field/version/coverage audit. TIGGE's reported 48-hour delay and noncommercial restrictions require current official rights/access verification before use; it is not an assumed free timely trading feed.

Price-only features: lagged returns/volatility, known calendar and seasonal terms. Further commodity/inventory/macro predictions require exact original release times and lagged vintages. Do not add revised inventory or full-day prices that arrived after the decision. Compare nested feature sets: price-only; + weather levels; + weather revisions; then additional predictions only in a separately recorded extension.

For stacked predictions, train base models on each training fold and generate downstream inputs only from earlier out-of-fold or forward predictions. In-sample fitted predictions, later model checkpoints, final data corrections and next-day weather observations cannot enter the meta-model as if historically available. Each horizon has its own target/eligibility mask; no synthetic labels for missing outcomes.

## Baselines, splits and bounded neural comparison
Use identical eligible dates/targets for no-change, seasonal mean, ridge, shallow tree and later small LSTM. Select hyperparameters on development/validation only. Initial proposal: one 16-32-unit unidirectional LSTM, fixed 20/60-session lookbacks, three prespecified seeds; at most12 architecture/lookback/regularization configurations and three seeds, or36neural training fits TOTAL across the entire study using one shared multi-horizon model; no per-horizon multiplication, then stop. A small causal TCN is a separately budgeted alternative, not an unlimited architecture search. Package/version/license/checkpoint review precedes downloads; no external model or weights were installed here. RTX4070 GPU/runtime/memory are unmeasured.

Once coverage is audited, record exact chronological cut dates before fitting: earliest60% complete years development, next20% validation, final20% locked test, if enough complete years exist. No random row split. Keep all same-date/contract-related records in one fold. Purge any training label interval overlapping a validation/test decision or outcome, at least the longest10-session horizon where needed, and record sequence warm-up and embargo rules. Lagged already-known validation history may be used at later decisions only as declared; never fit normalizers/imputation/feature selection on future blocks. Refits use expanding past data and only outcomes known by that fit time. Previously inspected weather years and cash-price results are development evidence, not an untouched holdout.

Define direction as future log price change >0 versus <=0 (exact zero belongs to the non-up class); retain ties separately in descriptive counts. Any directional model must emit a probability, with calibration fitted on development/validation only; constant training up-rate is the probability baseline. Report MAE/RMSE and directional Brier/calibration versus the strongest simple baseline by year, season and horizon, with paired time-block uncertainty that respects overlapping10-day labels. A small MAE gain, selected seed or weather-accuracy improvement is not profit. Retain all trials, missing cases and negative results. If the data span cannot support multiple independent seasons and a meaningful held-out period, stop at feasibility or forward capture rather than training a large net.

Only after forecast improvement survives evaluate a separately frozen decision rule against executable quotes: two-sided spread, fees, slippage, turnover, margin/capital assumptions, maturity/roll behavior, position overlap and fixed1/5/10-session holding rules. Report cost sensitivity and bench exposure; do not annualize a tiny selected sample into an impressive Sharpe. No actual orders, broker integration or automatic signal-weight change.

## Crop branch and other studies
Continue existing crop work as weather/crop-outcome research: finish corn-mask footprint and July31-Aug6future-week alignment, then define a state/region-compatible condition or yield target. One tiny SouthDakota grid sample cannot stand for statewide crop conditions; current zero eligible response samples cannot train a net. Acreage-weighted issued forecasts and crop stage must be genuinely available at cutoff. The monthly CPC/cash test was negative and partly elapsed/revised; keep that finding as a warning, not training proof.

First-five-minute SPY study remains separate with decision after09:35ET and future-session return only; daily commodity data cannot repair missing intraday trades/quotes. Don't concatenate incompatible time scales, asset exposures and outcome definitions simply to increase row count.
