# CSX annual audit conclusion — October 6, 2026

## Disposition
The collected cohort is usable for descriptive operational research with explicit limitations. It is NOT established as point-in-time data for a historical trading test. No modeling or trades started. This is a reviewed audit conclusion with unresolved source limitations, not a claim that all questions were answered.

## Verified collection
53 2025-dated workbooks plus the January 7, 2026 boundary report cover contiguous weekly intervals. The two November 19 URLs return identical bytes. Category labels/codes and terminal names are consistent across the 53-file cohort; selected numeric fields passed finite/nonnegative checks. Earlier reports document exact ranges, hashes and checks. Cross-year weeks remain whole weekly observations, not prorated calendar-year counts.

## Units and aggregation
CSX methodology Item 11 describes weekly originated/received counts and fertilizer as a Chemicals subgroup. It does not explicitly resolve container/trailer physical-unit versus railcar-equivalent counting. Keep Containers and Trailers as separate reported-count series with unit interpretation unverified; do not combine them with conventional carloads or convert to TEUs. Preserve originated and received separately. Never add fertilizer to Chemicals totals. Methodology also distinguishes Monday–Sunday Item 8 from Saturday–Friday measures. Do not generalize Rail Service E3/E4 dates to every sheet.
Source: https://www.stb.gov/wp-content/uploads/CSX_Methodology.pdf (Items 8, 11 and footnote 2).

## Availability and revisions
The 2025 CFR specifies Wednesday 5 PM Eastern submission, with next-business-day exceptions, and public posting. A submission obligation is not an observed posting timestamp. It also specifies a different generic Item 11 reporting week from CSX's methodology; preserve the source conflict and use each workbook's explicit dates rather than silently relabeling.
Source: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title49-vol9/pdf/CFR-2025-title49-vol9-sec1250-2.pdf (page 213, section 1250.1).

The STB index documents replacement/restatement of historical files, including other carriers. That establishes revision risk, not proof that these CSX observations were revised. Current downloads, filenames and Office modified times do not prove original public availability. Retrieval timestamps establish only when this project obtained the current bytes.
Source: https://www.stb.gov/reports-data/rail-service-data/ (correction notices).

## Next scope, not automatic execution
For historical return testing, obtain timestamped vintage evidence first; an assumed lag must be labeled as a sensitivity assumption and does not cure unknown revisions. Alternatively use prospective timestamped collection in a separately approved modeling scope. Preserve unresolved intermodal units; descriptive work can omit those series. No user purchase, account access or external contact is needed to finish this audit conclusion.
